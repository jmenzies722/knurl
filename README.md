# Knurl

Knurl is the live desk for Mac developers.

Your editor does the work. Knurl runs the room — music, volume, brightness,
speakers, and voice — so you stay in the hour instead of hunting for sliders.

A Hub. A side dial. A notch that grows out of the camera housing. Five
faces, nothing else.

```bash
swift test --parallel
scripts/run.sh
```

macOS 26+, Apple Silicon. This is a lab.

## The idea

You buy a Mac to build. Knurl is the thing that sits in the housing and
keeps the room: what’s playing, how loud it is, which speaker, whether
someone needs you. It is not a chatbot, not an agent dashboard, and not
another Control Center.

## Three places it lives

**The notch.** On a notched Mac this is home. At rest you see one live
fact — a track, a timer, Flow. Point at the housing and the dial drops
out of the bezel. Click for a shelf. Hold ⌃⌥M and the notch becomes
voice. Words land in the app you were in.

No notch? The same controls park in a pill above the Dock.

**The dial.** Turn it for Media, Volume, Bright, Output, or Mic. Click
to play, mute, or swap speakers. ⌃⌥K brings back the last face. ⌃⌥
arrows change volume from anywhere.

**The Hub.** Open it from the Dock or ⌘-tab. Six pages: Home, Tools,
Workspace, Flow, System, Sessions. Settings live here. Closing the
window does not quit Knurl.

## Voice

Hold ⌃⌥M, talk, let go. Knurl listens on this Mac, copies the words,
and pastes once. Nothing goes to the cloud.

## Phone

The iPhone app is the same crown, on the same Wi-Fi. Open Knurl on the
Mac first.

## Shortcuts

⌃⌥K — last face  
⌃⌥↑ / ⌃⌥↓ — volume  
⌃⌥M — hold to talk  
⌘1–⌘6 — Hub pages  
Escape — park
